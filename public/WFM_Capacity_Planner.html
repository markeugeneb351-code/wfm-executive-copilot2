Calculation Methodology

Offered load: calls × AHT ÷ open seconds.

Erlang C: the minimum integer productive agent count must meet both the selected service-level target and occupancy cap.

Service level: 1 − P(wait) × exp[−(agents − load) × answer-time threshold ÷ AHT].

ASA: P(wait) × AHT ÷ (agents − load). Occupancy: load ÷ agents.

Paid FTE: productive voice and nonvoice capacity is grossed up for shrinkage, then optional planning, contingency, and attrition allowances.

Gap: required paid FTE − available paid FTE. A positive result is a shortage. A negative result is a surplus.

Total FTE gap and inbound service level measure different things. Service level uses the inbound agents remaining after shrinkage and nonvoice allocation. A staffing surplus may correctly produce service level close to 100%.
